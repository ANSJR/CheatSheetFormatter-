Plain-text notes work as-is: ===== banners and ALL-CAPS lines become headings, ----+---- tables and space-aligned tables become real tables, ASCII drawings stay lined up, and lines wrapped at ~80 characters get re-joined. "Convert notes to Markdown" shows the cleaned-up version so you can fix anything by hand.
==text== highlights. A banner starting with BACK (or a line with only \pagebreak) starts side 2; \columnbreak starts a new column. Term: definition lines get the term colored, and in = formula [law: note] just the law name is colored. Tables can also be Markdown or pasted from Excel, Google Docs or Word. ![](graph.png) adds a picture from this folder.
Tap (or click) the sheet to put the cursor at that spot in your notes. Show on sheet (or Ctrl+Enter in the notes) goes the other way: it scrolls to the cursor's spot on the sheet and flashes it.
Zoom the sheet with the bar above it, or Ctrl + scroll (pinch on a trackpad) over the sheet. The menu stays the same size, and the zoom never changes the PDF. Drag the sheet with the left or middle mouse button to move around (the right button works too on Windows); a click without moving still jumps to your notes, and Shift + drag selects text.
